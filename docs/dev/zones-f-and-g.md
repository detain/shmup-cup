# Zones F and G: CELL VAULT, PRISM LABYRINTH and their bosses

How plan step **M2-13** replaced two more stub zones of the campaign with real ones: **zone F, CELL
VAULT** (organic cells: **chasing cells** and cells that divide into chasers when shot,
**regenerating tissue walls**, **grabbing tentacles**, spore sacs and the squid **MANTLE REGENT**,
whose tentacles curl in front of its eye to guard it) and **zone G, PRISM LABYRINTH** (crystal:
a **crystal labyrinth** of walls hanging from the ceiling and rising from the floor, four **seeded
cube rushes** that stack into walls, the second hidden bonus stage of the campaign, **GLIMMER
CACHE**, behind a **`ground` entrance**, and the crystal core **FACET MONARCH**, which hides its core
behind crystals and waves two tentacle arms like claws). Each zone brings its stage file, a roster,
a Direct-mode item plan, a stage and a boss song, a recoloured tileset and procedural placeholder
art; three new behaviours (`cell.chase`, `boss.squid`, `boss.facet`) carry them, the two bosses on
one shared **curling-arm** rule. **No engine change**: M2-09's turned parts (`spinPart`,
`setPartAngle`, circle hurtboxes) and M2-07's gimmicks were enough.

## Zone F: CELL VAULT

The inside of a living vault: a far wall of cells that **pulses** through its palette cycle and
**breathes** with a slow `wave`, fleshy folds scrolling in front of it, dark olive flesh with a
pale-green rim. Four checkpoints, the camera keys `0.8 → 0.7 → 0.75 → 1.3 → 0.75` px/tick. Times
are the bare scroll from the start (the bot's whole run with the boss is ≈ 220 s):

| Camera x | Time | Section |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 2,200 | 0 – 46 s | **The membrane** (checkpoint 0): `lymph-mote` streams, `chaser-cell`s alone and in formations, `mitosis-cell`s, `polyp-turret`s on the floor and ceiling, carriers (`tender`, zone A's capsule carrier) |
| 2,200 – 4,400 | 46 – 98 s | **The tissue passage** (checkpoint 2,200): seven regenerating tissue walls at world x 2,896–3,872 (the ship meets them ≈ 61–84 s in, [below](#the-regenerating-tissue-walls)); a ceiling polyp and a chaser before them, chasers and mote streams between them |
| 4,400 – 6,800 | 98 – 152 s | **The tentacle garden** (checkpoint 4,400): `vault-claw` grabbing tentacles on the floor and ceiling (five), hovering `spore-sac`s puffing `vault.spores`, a dividing cell, chaser formations |
| 6,800 – 8,400 | 152 – 172 s | **The pulse run** (checkpoint 6,800): 1.3 px/tick; mote streams of eight, chaser formations of four, dividing cells, a low spore sac |
| 8,400 – 9,100 | 172 – 188 s | **The calm**: exactly two carriers (the capsules for the boss) |
| 9,100 | ≈ 3:08 | The WARNING, then **MANTLE REGENT** (MR-06) |

**Backdrops.** `bg/vault-membrane` three times on the far layer (factor 0.2, `y` 0 / 64 / 128 — its
tile is 64 px tall and repeats seamlessly down as well as across) and `bg/vault-folds` on the mid
layer (0.45, `y` 152). The membrane is painted **only** in the four colours of `VAULT_RAMP`, which
the stage's `cycles` roll every 12 ticks, so the whole wall of cells pulses; a `wave` (rows 0–192,
amplitude 1, wavelength 40, period 150) makes it breathe.

### The regenerating tissue walls

Seven walls written as `tilemap.rle` rows over the generated heightfield: each is two tiles (16 px)
thick, 160 px from the next (world x 2,896, 3,056 … 3,856 — the last ends at 3,872), map rows 2–22.
The tile is the tileset's **`tissue`** (`hp` 3, `regen` 240, 10 points a cell — M2-07's
destructible terrain): shots break it cell by cell and a broken cell **grows back 240 ticks later**,
but **never into a ship** (the keep-out boxes of `DestructibleTerrain`); a checkpoint restart rolls
every broken cell back at once. **Every wall has a 56-px gap** (seven rows) at another height, so
the route zigzags; shooting a way through is the shortcut, and the regrowth closes it behind you.

The content test holds the walls to the corridor rule: ≥ 10 tissue columns, each with a gap of at
least 48 px (the stage-runtime test's flyable-corridor width — zone D's maze needed the same after
its gapless walls killed the bot, [zones-d-and-e.md](zones-d-and-e.md#the-destructible-maze)).

### The tentacle garden

`vault-claw` / `vault-claw-ceiling` are M2-07's **`tentacle.grab`** on the floor and on the ceiling:
a claw on a chain of eight links that, when a ship comes within `reach` 72 px of its anchor, lunges
(homing at `speed` 1.4, turning ≤ 8 units a tick, for 40 ticks) with a short pull on the ship
(`grabRadius` 36, `grabPull` 0.45), then lets go, retracts at 1.25 and rests 80 ticks. The claw
kills on contact like any enemy (there is no "held ship" state); five hits destroy it. The content
test holds the claws' `speed` and `retractSpeed` to 2 px/tick, like an aimed bullet's, since they
fly at the ship.

The **spore sacs** hover: a `waypoint` mover takes them to view (292, 56) — or (292, 144) for
`spore-sac-low` —, holds 320 ticks, then drifts off up or down (`leaveVy` ∓0.5), firing
`vault.spores` (a fan of three round purple spores, 56 units apart round the aim, at 0.75 px/tick,
then 170 ranked ticks + 40 of rest) from the right edge of the playfield
([balance](#balance-found-by-the-bot)).

The **squid** archetype: a long mantle trailing to the right, the great eye at its head end, two
tentacles pointing left above and below the eye. 40,000 points; `minion` is `chaser-cell`; the
WARNING reads `GIANT HOSTILE "MANTLE REGENT"` / `CLOSING IN - CODE MR-06`. It enters at view
(296, 100).

| Part | Sprite | Role |
|---|---|---|

| `mantle` | `bosses/regent-mantle` | Armour (`never`), 52 × 26 box at x 24 |
| `eye` | `bosses/regent-eye` | The **core**, 90 hp, 5,000 points (always vulnerable), at x −10 |
| `root-top`, `root-bottom` | `bosses/regent-root` | Attached to the mantle at (−12, ∓17): a tentacle's **root**, 24 hp, 1,500 points, a circle (`radius` 6) — **break it and the tentacle falls** (its chain goes with it) |

a root hung from the mantle (not an arm part), three segments and a tip, each attached to the one
before.

| Phase | Until | `boss.squid` tunables |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the eye below 60 hp, **or one root broken** (`partsDestroyed` of both roots, `count` 1) | track 0.3; curl 1 × sweep 30 (30 units a segment); guard 70, open 120; eye 3-ways of red ovals every 90 at 1.2 |
| 2 | the eye below 28 hp, or both roots broken | track 0.4; curl 1, sweep 30, guard 60, open 110; 3-ways every 80 at 1.25; tip needles every 100; one chasing cell launched every 200 ticks |
| 3 | — | track 0.5; curl 2 × sweep 15; guard 50, open 100; 5-ways (spread 36) every 70 at 1.3; tip needles every 80; a ring of 10 round purple bullets at 1.05 each time the tentacles open; one chasing cell every 180 |

"Break one = changes behaviour" (`shmup_feat.md` §13) is **data**: the first phase ends when either
root falls (`count` 1), and the next phase's script starts from wherever the arms are. While the
tentacles guard, the armoured segments sit in front of the eye and shots clink; the eye takes hits
while they are open. The bot takes ≈ 23.5 s.

checkpoints, the camera keys `0.8 → 0.7 → 0.75 → 1.3 → 0.75` (the bot's whole run ≈ 234 s):

| Camera x | Time | Section |
|---|---|---|

| 2,200 – 3,100 | 46 – 67 s | **The prism gallery** (checkpoint 2,200): four facet turrets, two on the floor and two on the ceiling, in the `ground` entrance's window x 2,300 – 3,100 — shoot them all and **GLIMMER CACHE** opens ([below](#the-prism-gallery-and-glimmer-cache)) |
| 3,100 – 4,600 | 67 – 103 s | **The crystal labyrinth**: seven solid crystal walls at world x 3,392–4,272 (the ship meets them ≈ 73–94 s in), hanging from the ceiling and rising from the floor in turn ([below](#the-crystal-labyrinth)); prism lenses high and low, a halo crystal |
| 4,600 – 6,800 | 103 – 152 s | **The cube rush** (checkpoint 4,600): four seeded `prism-cube` rushes of 8, 10, 10 and 8 cubes stacking onto 22 short crystal pillars ([below](#the-cube-rush)); motes, a geode, a low lens |
| 6,800 – 8,600 | 152 – 175 s | **The refraction run** (checkpoint 6,800): 1.3 px/tick over jagged spires; mote streams of eight, lenses, a turret, halo crystals, a geode |

| 9,300 | ≈ 3:10 | The WARNING, then **FACET MONARCH** (FM-07) |

**Backdrops.** `bg/prism-facets` three times on the far layer (0.2, `y` 0 / 64 / 128) and
`bg/prism-spires` on the mid layer (0.4, `y` 144). The facets take one of the four `PRISM_RAMP`
colours each (a position hash), so the `cycles` entry (every 20 ticks) makes them glint in turn; a
`haze` (rows 144–200, amplitude 1, wavelength 9, period 60) shimmers over the spires.

### The prism gallery and GLIMMER CACHE

The **second hidden bonus stage** of the campaign (zone B's PEARL GROTTO is the first) and the first
real one behind a **`ground` entrance** (M2-10's second kind — B's is a `gap`, so the two open
differently): the `bonus` event at x 2,300 (`"entrance": "ground"`, `until` 3,100,
`"stage": "glimmer-cache"`) arms its window when the camera reaches it; when the window **closes**
(camera ≥ 3,100) it opens if the players destroyed every ground enemy that appeared in it (at least
one) — the four gallery turrets at event x 2,340 (floor), 2,440 (ceiling), 2,620 (floor), 2,720
(ceiling). The warp then flies the players into GLIMMER CACHE with their score, lives and loadout;
the zone's clear is the bonus stage's `end` (FACET MONARCH is skipped); a death there sends them
back to the entrance with every entrance of the zone locked (RETRY STAGE resets that). An Arcade
checkpoint restart after the window armed disarms it — the camera goes back to 2,200 — and it arms
afresh at 2,300 with fresh counters: turrets shot before the death count for nothing.

**GLIMMER CACHE** (`glimmer-cache.stage.json`, type `bonus`, 1,800 px at 1 px/tick — half a minute,
the `terrain-prism` tileset, the zone's facet and spire bands, a faster palette cycle): six
formations of three `vault-carrier`s (M2-10's `bonus.enemies.json` — each leaves a gold bonus
capsule worth 1,000 points), a `vault-carrier-1up` (a 1UP), a glint mote, a cube rush of six, a
geode, and two static walls of `cube` blocks (16 × 72 px, one high at y 32, one low at y 96) to fly
round. It plays zone G's resident songs (it has none of its own).

**Why the turret before the gallery spawns at x 1,830** (the review finding). A `ground` window
weighs the ground kills made **while it is armed** against the ground enemies that **appeared while
it is armed**. The first build had a `facet-turret` at event x 2,000 (world x 2,400): when the
window armed at camera 2,300 it was still on screen, ahead of the ship, so shooting it counted as
one of the window's kills — the entrance opened with a gallery turret left standing (reproduced
headless from the zone's start: shoot everything but one ceiling turret, and the warp came). The
turret now spawns at event x 1,830 (world x 2,230) and has scrolled off and despawned (camera >
2,268) before the window arms. 1,830 kept both playtest runs as they were (234.1 s, no death);
1,820 cost the run without god mode two deaths by butterfly effect. The engine's counting rule
(M2-10) stayed as it was; the authoring rule is in
[`content/stages/README.md`](../../content/stages/README.md) and the content test now enforces it
([below](#the-content-checks-testintegrationcontenttestts)).

### The crystal labyrinth

Seven **solid** crystal walls (not destructible), written as `rle` rows with the tileset's wall-edge,
floor and ceiling tiles over the generated heightfield: each two tiles thick, 144 px from the next,
at world x 3,392, 3,536 … 4,256 (the last ends at 4,272). They hang from the ceiling (map rows 0–12)
and rise from the floor (rows 13–24) in turn, so the route zigzags up and down. The first wall was
moved to x 3,392 — past the gallery — by [the bot's runs](#balance-found-by-the-bot). The prism rock
is **lighter** than the facet wall behind it: with the first colours the walls vanished into the
backdrop.

### The cube rush

M2-07's **`cube.stack`**: each `formation` of `prism-cube`s is one **seeded** rush — every cube
moves to a random row (the gameplay RNG, `margin` 48 px from the edges), aims at the nearest ship and
flies at 1.5 px/tick on a `Ballistic` mover that stops at the terrain, where the tileset's **`cube`**
tile (`hp` 2) is placed in its cell — the rush **builds walls**. Four rushes (event x 4,760, 5,460,
5,960, 6,480; `drop: null` — a rush gives no capsule). Because the cubes aim at the ship, most of
them first flew off the left edge without stacking; **22 short crystal pillars** (eleven on the
floor, map rows 16–24, from world x 4,800; eleven on the ceiling, rows 0–8, from 4,880; 160 px
apart) now give them rock to stack on — 11 cubes stacked at once in the god-mode run, up from 1. A
checkpoint restart rolls the placed cubes back (`DestructibleTerrain`'s rollback).

## The new behaviours (`core/behaviors`)

All three follow the coroutine rules of D29 (sleep until the next thing to do, whole-tick timers,
no allocation per wake). Defaults in brackets; the zone content overrides many of them.

### `cell.chase`

The chasing cell. It drifts in along its row to the left at [`speed` 1.1] px/tick for [`enterTicks`
50] ticks, then chases the nearest living player — a `Homing` mover at `speed`, turning at most
[`turnRate` 6] binary units a tick — for [`chaseTicks` 150] ticks, then swims straight on along its
last heading (a `Homing` mover with turn rate 0: it keeps its course without a jump, like
`rocket.homing`). A cell thrown out of a dividing cell (the `bubble.split` parent set a `Straight`
mover on it) first flies out for [`scatterTicks` 24] ticks instead of drifting in; one launched by
a boss (`BossScriptApi.launch` — MANTLE REGENT's) drifts in from where it was launched. It never
fires; its body is the danger. Three wakes in its life.

With four directions the chase is dodgeable: the turn cap keeps it from following a ship that
changes lanes, the time limit ends the chase, and the straight run after it is predictable. A
`speed` of 0 or less is 1 px/tick, tick counts below 1 are one tick, `turnRate` is floored and below
0 is 0.

### The curling-arm rule

Both bosses curl **arms**: an arm segment is a circle-hit part (`radius` > 0) attached to another
part (`isArm`); an arm's **root** is its first segment — the one whose parent is not an arm segment
(MANTLE REGENT's tentacle root hangs from the mantle, FACET MONARCH's from the body). The helpers:

| Helper | What it does |
|---|---|
| `armSide(parts, i)` | The side a segment curls to, from its root's rest offset: a root above its parent (`restY` < 0) curls counter-clockwise (−1), one below it clockwise (+1). The arms point left, so "curling in" brings both towards their parent's row — **mirrored above and below** |
| `curlArms(api, speed)` | `spinPart` on every standing segment at `speed × side` units a tick: every segment turns by the **same amount relative to its parent**, so the arm curls more towards its tip, like a tentacle |
| `setArmCurl(api, units)` | `setPartAngle` on every standing segment to `units × side` — what a sweep ends on, so a sweep that started from a fraction of its step (a phase change mid-sweep) lands exactly |
| `armCurl(api)` | The curl now: the first standing segment's angle, measured towards its side, in `[−512, 512)` units |

A phase **carries the curl on** from where the last one left it (`armCurl`), turning at its own speed
and setting each turn point exactly when it arrives (`setArmCurl`), so a phase change **never makes
an arm jump**. The test round found that a phase used to clamp the curl to its own sweep — a phase
with a smaller sweep than the curl the last one left (or a squid phase after a facet wave had curled
the arms away from the core's row) snapped the arms at its first turn point, jumps of 50, 42 and 67
units in the tests; the arms now turn from wherever they are, however far. The shipped phases never
exceed their sweeps, so no golden changed.

M2-09's part transforms place each child by its parent's world angle, so turning every segment by
the same relative amount is all a tentacle needs.

### `boss.squid`

MANTLE REGENT's script. Tunables: [`trackSpeed` 0.35, `margin` 48] (follows the nearest player's
height, `margin` px inside the playfield); the tentacle cycle — straight for [`openTicks` 100],
curling in at [`curl` 1] units a tick per segment for [`sweepTicks` 48] until the tips meet in
front of the core, holding for [`guardTicks` 60], uncurling in `sweepTicks`; [`fireTicks` 70,
`ways` 3, `spread` 40, `bulletSpeed` 1.3] (aimed spreads of red ovals from every standing core);
[`gunTicks` 0 = never] (an aimed pink needle from every standing gun — the tips); [`ring` 0 = none,
`ringSpeed` 1] (a ring of round purple bullets from the cores **every time the tentacles open**, each
turned half a gap from the last); [`launchTicks` 0 = never, `count` 1] (the boss's `minion`, `count`
per standing core, rank-scaled).

One script per phase, sleeping until the soonest of four timers (the tentacles, the spreads, the
tips, the launches). A phase starts by **uncurling** from wherever the last one left the arms; seen
from outside every hold of the cycle lasts one tick longer than its timer (a sweep reaches its end
on its last tick, where the next state begins). `curl` is rounded (at least 1), the timers below 1
are one tick. A broken tentacle leaves the other's cycle exactly as it was.

### `boss.facet`

FACET MONARCH's script. Tunables: [`trackSpeed` 0.3, `margin` 50]; the wave — every segment turns
at [`wave` 1] units a tick, [`waveTicks` 40] from straight to a turn point, so each arm sweeps from
`wave × waveTicks` units curled away from the core's row to as much curled towards it and back
(turn points `2 × waveTicks` apart; the arms open and close like a pair of claws); [`fireTicks` 90,
`ways` 1, `spread` 32, `bulletSpeed` 1.25] (aimed purple needles from every standing gun — the
tips); [`ring` 0 = none, `ringTicks` 150, `ringSpeed` 1] (rings of round red bullets from every
standing core — an armoured core fires too — each turned half a gap); [`laserTicks` 0 = never,
`laserLength` 384, `laserWidth` 6, `telegraph` 50, `active` 40] (a telegraphed lane laser to the
left from each standing core, **detached** — left where it was fired).

A phase takes the arms on from wherever the last left them towards the curled-in turn point (the
other one when they are there already or past it). `wave` is rounded (at least 1), `waveTicks` below
1 is one tick; one lane at a time while `laserTicks` outlasts a lane (telegraph + grow + active +
fade).

## Gotchas

| Symptom | Cause / fix |
|---|---|
| A `ground` entrance opens with one of its targets still standing | A ground enemy of an **earlier** event was still up when the window armed and was shot in place of one of the window's own. Keep ground enemies out of the screen at the window's `x` (the content test plays every `ground` stage and checks it) |
| GLIMMER CACHE never opens although all four turrets are down | It opens when the window **closes** (camera x 3,100), not at the fourth kill; after a death in the bonus stage every entrance of the zone stays locked (RETRY STAGE resets that); after an Arcade restart the turrets shot before the death count for nothing |
| Every shot at a boss core clinks | An armour hurtbox covers it — FACET MONARCH's first build hung the armour on the housing. Give armour its own part behind the core |
| An arm jumps when a phase starts | It must not: a phase carries the curl on from `armCurl`. A behaviour that clamps the inherited curl to its own sweep, or places arm parts (`setPartOffset`) instead of turning them, makes them jump |
| One arm curls the wrong way | `armSide` reads the **root's** `restY`: a root hung above its parent curls down, below it up. A root placed on the parent's row (`y` 0) counts as below |
| A tissue wall "never grows back" | It will not grow into a ship's box — fly away from the hole; a checkpoint restart rebuilds it at once |
| The cube rush builds nothing | Cubes aimed at a ship with no rock behind it fly off the left edge; stacking needs terrain in their path (the pillars) |
| The content test flags a claw's `speed` | Claws and chasing cells fly at the ship, so their `speed` (and a claw's `retractSpeed`) are held to 2 px/tick like an aimed bullet |
| Every golden fails after adding a sprite or a behaviour | The sorted sprite and script ids moved; re-bless (`pnpm golden:update`) and check that inputs, ticks and outcomes are unchanged |
| An allocation guard of a boss behaviour fails at ≈ 66 KB under load | Load noise: the squid / facet guards take the best of five windows, and `behaviors-facet-alloc` still came in at 66,736 bytes now and then (in the full suite, or beside the squid guard) — rerun it alone; a real per-tick allocation fails in every window, alone too |
| The Tizen bundle check fails on size | 320.3 of 350 KB gzip after M2-13; the content is inlined — trim or wait for M2-17's budget work |
