# Advanced bosses: captains, raids, double and inner bosses, timers, the HP bar, boss rushes

How plan step **M2-09** gave the boss system the Darius-style variety of `shmup_feat.md` §13:
**turned parts** (binary-angle transforms, circle hurtboxes, heading frames), **mid-bosses**
("captains") that ride the scrolling camera until destroyed, **battleship raids** larger than the
screen with boss-relative camera segments, a **boss inside a boss**, **double bosses** that take
turns (the survivor enrages), **boss timers** (an escape after the limit — an ending flag), the
**boss HP bar** in the top HUD bar (a display option) and **boss-rush** stages. `core/bosses` is
now `implemented`.

It all sits on the M1-13 boss system — the boss section of the enemies data, parts, weak points,
phases, the WARNING and the death sequence — described in
[bosses-and-warning.md](bosses-and-warning.md). This page is the *how and why* of what M2-09
added and the map of the step. Exact signatures are in

the TSDoc in `packages/core/src/{bosses,behaviors,data,stage,world,weapons,ui,scenes,config,events,save,debug}/index.ts`

## Four boss slots

`BossSystem.slots` holds **`MAX_BOSSES` 4** `Boss` slots (slot 0 is still `bosses.boss`), each
with its 16 `BossPart`s. Four covers the worst case the plan names: a double boss (two slots), a
captain beside it and an inner boss revealed while another slot still runs its death sequence.

- **Part slots and ids.** A part's **part slot** is `BossPart.global = owner × 16 + index`
  (`BOSS_PART_SLOTS` 64); its hit / grid / laser-source id `BossPart.slot` is
  `BOSS_PART_ID_BASE` (64) + that. `MAX_HIT_TARGETS` grew 80 → **128**, the weapons' boss-part
  cooldown tables to `PIERCE_TABLES` × 64 (`WeaponSystem.partCooldowns`), the World's
  `laserSources` to the 64 enemies + 64 parts (`bosses.parts`, flat). `damagePart(index, …)` and
  `isArmoured(index)` take the **part slot** — for boss slot 0 that is still the part's own index,
  so M1 code and tests keep working. `WeaponHost.bosses` reads `bosses.parts` (it read
  `bosses.boss.parts`).
- **The main encounter.** Stage bosses (role `boss`) still come **one at a time**: a `warning` or
  `boss` event for a stage boss is ignored while one is in play (`BossSystem.mainActive`). Bosses
  that belong to the encounter — a partner, an inner boss — take other slots; **the last stage
  boss to end** (death or escape, no other stage boss in play, a dying one included) releases the
  scroll lock and sets `stageClear` — or brings the next rush boss.
- **Free slots.** `startWarning` / `startBoss` put a boss in the lowest slot that is `None` or
  `Dead`; with every slot busy they return `false` (a partner or inner boss without a slot simply
  does not come — the leader fights alone, the outer boss ends the encounter).
- **Entries inside `update()`.** A boss that enters during phase 3 from another slot's clock (the
  WARNING's end, a partner, an inner boss) records `Boss.enterTick` and starts counting its intro
  on the **next** tick — so a partner's intro ends on the same tick as its leader's, whichever slot
  number it got.

## Turned parts

A part may turn: `angle` (its turn relative to its parent, binary units — 1024 per turn,
clockwise on screen, y down) and `spin` (units per tick; a behaviour may set both —
`setPartAngle`, `spinPart`, `aimPart`). Every tick, `move()` adds `spin` to `angle` (not while
dying: the spins freeze), and `place()`, parents first, computes each part's **world angle** (its
parent's + its own, wrapped into `[0, 1024)`) and puts it at its parent's centre plus its local
offset **turned by the parent's world angle**:

```text
x = parent.x + localX · cos(parentAngle) − localY · sin(parentAngle)
y = parent.y + localX · sin(parentAngle) + localY · cos(parentAngle)
```

The sines come from a module-level `Float64Array` copy of the committed table (`SIN`, a quarter
turn of overhang so `SIN[a + 256]` is the cosine) — no call per part, and a zero turn keeps the
M1 translation exactly (`p.x + localX`). A spinning hub therefore carries a ring of pods, and a
destroyed parent still takes its children with it (the cascade is by index).

**Sprites are not rotated.** Pixi's transform setters allocate (M2-02's bending lasers found it),
so a turned part shows its heading with **heading frames**: `turn` (2–64) frames whose frame `k`
points at `k × 1024 / turn` units; `move()` picks the frame nearest the world angle
(`turnedFrame(angle, frames)`, written out inline in the hot loop). Round art (`bosses/orb`,
`bosses/core`) needs no frames at all. `turn` and `anim` are exclusive.

**Hit as circles.** A box never turns, so a turned part is hit as a **circle** — `radius`
(1–128) instead of a `hurtbox` (the loader refuses both, and refuses `turn` on a part with a box).
`BossPart.radius` > 0 marks it; `hw = hh = radius` give the grid its square (the broad phase),
then the narrow phases test the circle: the shots' grid visitor (`core/weapons` `visitPart`) takes
the shot box's nearest point to the centre, the ship contact is circle against circle. A core may
have a radius instead of a box.

**`aimPart(i, maxStep)`** — a turret: the heading comes from the bullet system's `aimFrom` (the
same quantisation as aimed shots — 16 or 32 directions by difficulty), so a turret's barrel and
its bullets agree. The part's own `angle` changes by at most `maxStep` units (≤ 0 = at once)
toward it; its world angle and heading frame follow in the next phase 5. It returns the new world
heading, or -1 (a bad index, nobody to aim at).

## Captains (mid-bosses)

A boss section with **`role: "captain"`** (`BossRole.Captain` 1; `BOSS_ROLES` in `core/data`):

- enters with a stage **`boss`** event only — a `warning` naming a captain is a content issue
  (the reference pass) and `startWarning` refuses one; it takes any free slot, also while a stage
  boss fights;
- **rides the scrolling camera** like a regular enemy group would: no brake, no scroll lock, no
  change of music (the stage theme keeps playing), no `bossWarning` status;
- has a **short death sequence** — chain explosions until `CAPTAIN_CHAIN_TICKS` 48, then a blast
  (`FX ExplosionLarge` + `BossChain`, `SFX BossExplode`, a **medium** shake of
  `CAPTAIN_BLAST_SHAKE_TICKS` 20 — no flash, no hit-stop, no rumble), the tally on 49
  (`CAPTAIN_TALLY_TICKS`: its score to the killer, `BossDefeated`, **no** jingle), `Dead` on 72
  (`CAPTAIN_CLEAR_TICKS`) — and **never clears the stage** or releases a lock; the bullets are
  cancelled into points as for any boss;
- may not have a `raid`, `partner`, `inner` or `alternate` (load issues); a `timeLimit` and a
  `minion` are fine — a captain's escape sets no ending flag;
- is counted by the HP bar only when no stage boss is.

The four archetypes are boss behaviours in `core/behaviors` (tunables in the module docs and
[below](#behaviours)): `captain.ram`, `captain.launcher`, `captain.circler`, `captain.crab`.

## Battleship raids

A boss with a **`raid`** (`BossRaidSpec`: 1–16 `segments` of `x`, `y`, `ticks` (default 120),
`hold` (default 0), and `loop`, default `true`) is a battleship larger than the screen that the
camera flies around:

1. **Anchored.** When it enters, `Boss.anchored` is set and its origin is placed from the camera
   position of that tick (`anchorX` / `anchorY`) instead of the moving camera — it stays at that
   world point. A `boss` event stops the camera at once (`stage.brake(0)`); after a WARNING the
   brake has already stopped it.
2. **The fight starts the path** (`startRaid`): the camera is locked (`brake(0)`), the system's
   `RaidCamera` target is set to the camera, and the stage runner is told to follow it —
   `StageRunner.follow(target)`. In free flight (no runner) the system steers the camera's
   velocity instead (`BossCamera.vx` / `vy` — the World moves the camera by it).
3. **Every phase 3** (`steerRaid`, before the stage runner) moves the target: segment `s` eases
   (in-out quad, written out) from the previous offset to `(x, y)` over `ticks`, then holds for
   `hold` ticks, and the target is the **boss's origin + that offset** — the offsets are the
   camera's top-left corner minus the boss's origin, so the camera keeps following a boss that
   moves. After the last segment it loops to the first (or stays with `loop: false`); a
   `ticks: 0` segment jumps.
4. **The runner's step 3** puts the camera on the target and records `dx` / `dy` as usual, so the
   ships, their shots and the bullets ride along; the stage length still caps x. **The timeline
   waits** where the follow began — no camera key, event, checkpoint or trigger disarm past that
   x while following ([stage-runtime.md](stage-runtime.md#following-a-target-m2-09)). A pan over
   the stage's `end` right after the boss therefore clears nothing, and the spawns beyond it are
   not used up.
5. **Death or escape** eases the camera back — from where it is to where the raid began
   (`raidHomeX` / `raidHomeY`) over `RAID_RETURN_TICKS` (120 — the death chain, so the camera is
   home at the final blast) or `BOSS_ESCAPE_TICKS` (90), landing exactly there; the tick after,
   `follow(null)` hands the camera back and the timeline carries on from that x. The lock is
   released with the encounter's end as for any stage boss.

**Firing from view.** `place()` refreshes `BossPart.inView` for an anchored boss — its centre
within the view grown by `BOSS_FIRE_MARGIN` (8 px) — and `canFire(i)` (so every fire primitive
and `launch`) answers `false` for a raid part off screen; the snapshot keeps the arithmetic out of
the cold script path. The raid's turrets run `boss.raid`. `clear()` (a checkpoint restart) ends
the follow, and `restartAt` forgets any target anyway.

**IRON LEVIATHAN** (RL-01, `raid-range`) is five 96-px `bosses/raid-hull` sections (480 px — wider
than the 384-px view), seven 12-hp turrets with 16 heading frames (four on top, three below) and
a 60-hp `reactor` core that takes damage only once the two turrets nearest it are gone; four
camera segments that fly over it, along it and back, looping; a 5,400-tick time limit; LEVIATHAN
HEART inside.

## Double bosses

A boss with a **`partner`** (another stage boss without a partner or raid of its own) brings it
in with its own intro, in another slot (`enterPartner`); the two slots point at each other
(`Boss.partner`), the entry that names the partner is the **leader** (`Boss.leader`).

- **Turns** (`alternate` ticks, needs a partner). At the fight's start the leader arms its turn
  clock (`turnTicks`) and the follower **withdraws** at once. At every turn the fighting one
  withdraws and the resting one comes forward: an eased move over `BOSS_TURN_TICKS` (60) to
  `BOSS_REST_X` (the playfield's right edge + 40 — mostly off screen) or back home. While resting
  it is drawn from **`bosses.backBatch`** (`LayerId.GroundEnemies`, behind the air layer), is not
  in the grid, cannot be hit or touched, and its script and phase clock wait (`runScript` skips a
  resting or turning slot; `phaseTicks` stands still — its time-limit clock does not). The motion
  it had before the turn is kept (`restMotion`) and resumes when it is home again. Attached lasers
  are detached at a turn. A partner still flying in when the first turn comes (its intro longer
  than `alternate`) only delays the turns by another `alternate` — the M2-09 test pass fixed a bug
  where they stopped for good.
- **Enrage.** When one of the pair starts its death sequence the survivor **enrages**
  (`enrage`, `BossEnrageSpec`): it comes forward for good (the turns stop), `fireWait` intervals ×
  `fireRate` (default 0.625, at least 1 tick), `track` / `orbit` speeds × `speed` (default 1.5), and
  a jump to `phase` when that is later than the running one (a survivor still in its intro starts
  its fight in that phase). `BossScriptApi.enraged` tells a behaviour.
- **The pair's music and clear.** The boss music fades and the jingle plays only for the **last**
  of the pair; the stage clears after both. The HP bar counts both. The leader's escape takes the
  partner along (resting or not).
- **Ended slots.** When either slot ends (its death sequence over, or escaped) the link is cut
  **both ways** (`unlink`), and every lookup of the mate checks the link both ways (`mateOf`) — a
  captain or rush boss that later takes the dead partner's slot is never enraged, turned or taken
  along (a review fix of M2-09).

**The EMBER AND FROST TWINS** (TE-01 + TF-02, `twin-range`): two small hull blocks with a 30-hp
radius core and a gun each, above and below the middle, taking 300-tick turns; `boss.hover`
(3-way spreads) until the core is below 15, then `boss.lanes`; enrage `fireRate` 0.5, `speed` 1.6,
`phase` 1 (the lanes at once).

## Boss inside a boss

A boss with an **`inner`** boss (a stage boss; the chain may not loop) reveals it at its **final
blast**: the inner boss enters another slot with its intro starting at the outer's **first core**
(its position relative to the camera, `Boss.startX` / `startY`) and easing to its own home. The
outer's tally still pays; its music fade, jingle and the stage clear wait for the inner boss
(which counts as the main encounter). A raid's camera is already home at the blast, so the heart
appears where the player sees the reactor explode. With no free slot the outer ends the encounter
alone. **LEVIATHAN HEART** (LH-02) is a 40-hp spinning core with two armoured orbs circling it,
running `boss.hover`.

## Timers and escapes

A boss with a **`timeLimit`** (60–36,000 fight ticks) escapes when `Boss.fightTicks` (counted in
the fight only — never during the intro) reaches it:

- `startEscape`: the new state **`BossState.Escape` (6)** — no script, no hits (the parts leave
  the grid; a shot flies on), no contact, lasers detached; the boss eases over
  `BOSS_ESCAPE_TICKS` (90) to its entry's **intro start past the right edge** (an inner boss too —
  not back to where it was revealed, a review fix); a raid's camera starts home; a stage boss's
  music fades when no other stage boss fights; its partner escapes with it. It is still drawn and
  still counted by the HP bar.
- `finishEscape` (90 ticks later): `Dead` with `Boss.escaped`, **`SimEventKind.BossEscaped`
  (14)** (`id` = enemy index, `x` / `y` = its origin, `param` 0), **no tally**; a stage boss sets
  **`EndingFlag.BossEscaped`** (bit 1) in the World's new **`endingFlags`** (hashed; since M2-10 the
  campaign's `RunState.noteWorldEnd` carries it between zones as the run flag `bossEscaped` and the
  ending selection reads it) and ends the encounter like a
  death (lock released, `stageClear` or the next rush boss).

A boss already dying never escapes. IRON LEVIATHAN escapes after 90 s of fight — the
`raid-range-escape` golden flies it without power-ups.

## The boss HP bar

**The model** (`core/bosses` `BossHpBar`, `world.bosses.hpBar`, refreshed in `sync()`):
`visible`, `hp`, `maxHp`, `bosses` (how many are counted). It counts the **stage bosses** in
their intro, fight, escape or death sequence until the blast — or, when there is none, the
**captains**. Each contributes the hit points that stand between the players and the kill: its
cores and the parts a core requires, transitively (`CompiledBoss.barMask` — armour and
decoration never count). A boss in its intro contributes its full strength × the intro's progress
(the bar fills up as it flies in), a dying one 0 (the bar empties at the kill and disappears at
the blast).

**The view** (`core/ui`): `bossHpBarFill(bar, width)` → `⌈hp × width / maxHp⌉` whole pixels, at
most `width`, 0 when hidden or empty — any hit point left shows at least 1 px, full only at full
strength. `buildHud(world, list, sprites, bossHp)` draws, while `bossHp` and `hpBar.visible`,
`BOSS` (red `0xf85858`, string slot 22) at x 148 and a 4-px frame `BOSS_HP_BAR_WIDTH` (64) wide at
x 176 with a 2-px red fill of `bossHpBarFill(bar, 62)` **in place of the hi-score** (`HI` and its
number return when the bar goes). `HUD_STRING_COUNT` is 23, `HUD_COMMAND_COUNT` 100. `Hud` has
`showBossHp` and rebuilds only when the fill's pixel count (or the option) changes.

**The option.** `UserOptions.display.bossHpBar` (default **off** — neither source game had one;
save format 1, no migration: an older save resolves it to `false`), the Options row **BOSS HP**
(`OptionsItem.BossHp` 9 — **BACK is 10**, the panel 288 × 192 at y 12 for eleven rows), a toggle
that pushes `UserOptionKind.BossHpBar` (9, 1 / 0) live. No host applies that event: the scene
flow copies the **saved** value into `game.hud.showBossHp` on every displayed frame, so the HUD
follows it once the Options screen closes. Details:
[saves-and-options.md](saves-and-options.md), [scenes-and-ui.md](scenes-and-ui.md#the-hud).

## Boss rushes

A stage of **`type: "bossRush"`** (default `normal`, `STAGE_TYPES`) runs its **`rush`** list
(1–16 `StageRushEntry`s: `enemy` — a stage boss —, `delay` default 60, `warning` default `false`):

- `createBossSystem` compiles the list into typed arrays; `rushIndex` (the entry that comes or
  runs next) and `rushDelay` (ticks until it comes, -1 = none waiting) are hashed.
- At the end of every `update()`, while **no stage boss** is in play (captains do not hold it,
  a dying stage boss does), `rushDelay` counts down; at 0 the entry starts with `startWarning` or
  `startBoss`. An entry that cannot start (only possible without validation) is skipped.
- When the entry's encounter ends (its death sequence, an inner boss or partner included, or an
  escape), the next entry waits its delay; after the **last** one the stage is clear. The
  stage-clear jingle plays only at the last tally.
- A `bossRush` stage has **no `end` event** (the loader refuses one — its last boss ends it); its
  camera path, spawns and checkpoints work as usual. A checkpoint restart (`clear()`) brings the
  **current** entry again after its delay.

**GAUNTLET RANGE** (`gauntlet-range`): TRIAL WARDEN with its WARNING after 360 ticks, LEVIATHAN
HEART 120 ticks after it, the twins with their WARNING 120 ticks after that.

| `boss.raid` | `fireTicks` 50, `bulletSpeed` 1.5, `ways` 1, `spread` 32, `aimStep` 0 | A raid's turrets: every volley each standing gun that may fire (on screen) turns to the nearest player (`aimPart`) and fires a pink `ways`-way along its new heading |

**Script API additions** (`BossScriptApi`): `enraged`; `setPartAngle(i, angle)` (wrapped, NaN
ignored), `spinPart(i, speed)`, `partAngle(i)` (whole units), `aimPart(i, maxStep)`;
`orbit(cx, cy, rx, ry, speed)` (the new `BossMotion.Orbit` 3 — from the ellipse angle of where the
boss is, × the enrage factor; 0 / NaN speed = hold); `launch(i)` (the `minion` at the part's
centre through `BossHost.enemies.spawn`; `false` without a minion, when the part may not fire or
no enemy slot is free — allocates the minion's coroutine, as every spawn does); `canFire(i)` is
now also `false` while resting and for a raid part off screen; `fireWait` includes the enrage
factor. Positions (`moveTo`, `track`, `orbit`) are relative to the boss's **anchor**: playfield
pixels, or — for a raid — the world point where it entered.

## The World, the stage runner and the view

- `createWorld` passes the stage to `createBossSystem(world, behaviors, stageSpec)` (its `rush`);
  `laserSources` lists every part slot; `view.batches` gained `bosses.backBatch` right before
  `bosses.batch`; the World has `endingFlags` (0; `BossHost.endingFlags`).
- `BossHost` gained `enemies.spawn` (a captain's minions), `endingFlags`, `stage.follow` and the
  camera's `vx` / `vy` (`BossCamera` — free-flight raids).
- `StageRunner.follow(target)` / `following` and `StageCameraTarget` — see
  [stage-runtime.md](stage-runtime.md#following-a-target-m2-09).
- The render contract did not change: resting parts are an ordinary sprite batch on the
  ground-enemy layer. The debug overlay's boss outlines cover every slot (`OUTLINE_CAPACITY.boss`
  64 × 4; a circle part as its bounding square).

## Determinism, hashing and golden replays

`hashWorld`'s `mixBosses` now walks every slot: its state (and nothing more for an empty slot),
spec index, role, position and anchor, intro start, timers (state, phase, fight), escape flag,
script + wake tick, motion and its parameters (move-to, track, orbit, `afterMove`), destroyed
mask, killer, blast flag, the pair (partner, resting, turning, turn clock, enrage), the raid
(raiding, returning, segment, ticks, from x / y), rush entry, part count, and per part its offset,
position, **angle, spin**, hit points, flags and flash; then the WARNING, **the raid camera
target**, **the rush index and delay** and **`world.endingFlags`**. The part cooldown tables are
hashed with 64 part slots each. The runner's follow target is not in its state: its owner (the raid
camera) is hashed. The turned placement reads the committed sine table, so it is identical on
every engine.

The goldens were **re-blessed once** (`8cdc9eb`): the hash layout changed (four slots, their new
fields, the 64-slot part tables, the rush state, the ending flags) and the new content shifts the
sorted sprite and script ids — every replay's inputs, tick count and outcome stayed identical
(only `hashes` / `finalHash` changed). The test round added four replays — `captain-range-god`,
`raid-range-god` (full loadout: the raid and the heart shot down), `raid-range-escape` (no
power-ups: the escape and `EndingFlag.BossEscaped`) and `twin-range-god` (turns, enrage) — and
`golden.test.ts` checks what each of them went through (which slots ran, escaped, enraged; the
camera handed back). The older files are byte-identical since.

## Zero allocation and the hot-path rules

Guards: `bosses-advanced-alloc.test.ts` (a raid held in its fight — the camera path, turned
turrets, circle hits by the Laser and the Options; a double boss's turns; a captain's orbit with
spinning orbs while the stage scrolls) and `bosses-escape-alloc.test.ts` (a raid escaping and its
camera easing back), each under 64 KB over thousands of ticks in its own worker.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Sines read from a module-level `Float64Array` (`SIN`) by whole index | A `sinB` call per part per tick would pass and return fractions |
| `turnedFrame`, the eases of the raid path and return, and the orbit are written out inline | Fractional arguments / results of a call V8 does not inline are boxed |
| `canFire` reads the phase-5 snapshot `inView`; `hasTarget()` has no arithmetic; `aimPart` hands the fractional aim to the bullet system's hot `aimFrom` and only moves whole numbers | Cold script code runs in V8's lower tiers, which box every double they compute |
| `RaidCamera` is a class with two fields; the runner reads its fields, never copies | A fractional object read every tick must keep unboxed fields (the M1-08 camera lesson) |
| Rush entries, raid segments, enrage factors are compiled into typed arrays / `CompiledBoss` fields | The tick never reads content objects |
| Resting bosses go to a second preallocated batch (`backBatch`), not a reordered one | No array work per frame; the renderer's bindings stay put |

## Using it headlessly

```ts
import { BossState, EndingFlag, createGame, createHeadlessPlatform, hashWorld } from '@shmup/core';

const game = createGame(createHeadlessPlatform(), { stage: 'raid-range', loadout: 'full' }, db);
game.world.debugFlags.godMode = true;
const { bosses } = game.world;
while (bosses.boss.state !== BossState.Fight) game.step(); // WARNING, then a 240-tick intro
game.world.stage?.following === bosses.raidCamera; // true: the camera follows the raid
bosses.hpBar; // { visible: true, hp, maxHp, bosses: 1 } — the reactor and the turrets it needs
bosses.defeat(0); // a tool: every core destroyed → the death sequence, the camera eases home
while (bosses.slots[1].state !== BossState.Fight) game.step(); // LEVIATHAN HEART, revealed
// …or let the time limit run out instead: the escape, then
game.world.endingFlags & EndingFlag.BossEscaped; // 1
hashWorld(game.world); // covers every slot, the raid camera, the rush and the ending flags
```

`bosses.startBoss(db.enemyIndex.get('captain-circler')!)` brings a captain in beside anything
(tests, free flight); `?scene=flight&stage=raid-range` flies a stage without the scene flow.

## Gotchas

| Symptom | Cause / fix |
|---|---|
| `damagePart(i)` hits the wrong boss | It takes the **part slot** (`owner × 16 + index`), not the part index — for slot 0 they are equal |
| `boss.warning … is a captain` / `startWarning` returns `false` for a mid-boss | Captains fly in with a `boss` event only |
| A second stage boss's `boss` event did nothing | The main encounter runs one stage boss at a time; make it a `partner`, an `inner` boss or a rush entry — or a captain |
| A raid's spawns after the boss never came during the fight | Intended: the timeline waits where the follow began and resumes after the camera is back |
| The camera jumped when the raid started | The first segment has `ticks: 0`, or the boss moved far from where it entered before its fight |
| A raid turret never fires | It is off screen (`inView`) — or it is not a `gun`; `boss.raid` only fires from guns |
| A turned part's box hurtbox is refused | Boxes never turn: use `radius`; and `turn` needs a round hurtbox or none |
| The sprite of a spinning part does not turn | Sprites are not rotated: give it `turn` heading frames, or use round art |
| The resting twin cannot be hit | Intended: a resting boss is out of the grid; it comes forward at the next turn (or for good when its partner dies) |
| A double boss never turns | No `alternate`, the partner could not get a slot (fights alone), or one of them is enraged |
| The stage did not clear after the outer boss died | Its inner boss is the encounter now; the clear comes after it |
| `stageClear` on a boss-rush stage too early | The last entry's encounter ended; check the `rush` list — a `bossRush` stage has no `end` event |
| The HP bar shows a captain's strength during a stage boss's WARNING | The bar falls back to captains while no stage boss is in its intro / fight / escape / death |
| The HP bar is full at once, not filling up | The boss has `introTicks: 0` |
| The BOSS HP row changes nothing until BACK | The HUD reads the saved option; the live `UserOption` event has no consumer |
| A golden fails after touching the boss system | Every new field that changes the simulation is hashed — re-bless intentionally with the reason in the commit |
