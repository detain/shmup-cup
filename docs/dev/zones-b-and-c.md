# Zones B and C: BRINE NEBULA, DUNE EXPANSE and their bosses

How plan step **M2-11** replaced two stub zones of the campaign with real ones: **zone B, BRINE
NEBULA** (bubbles and water: splitting bubbles, a fish inside a bubble, wavy raster water, the
mid-boss **SPUME HERALD**, the boss **GALVANIC MAW** and the first hidden bonus stage of a real
zone, **PEARL GROTTO**) and **zone C, DUNE EXPANSE** (desert: sand worms bursting from the dunes,

## Zone B: BRINE NEBULA

A teal-and-violet nebula over a drifting "sea" that wobbles with a `wave` raster effect. Four
checkpoints, the camera keys `0.75 → 0.7 → 0.6 → 0.8 → 1.4 → 0.75` px/tick. Times are the bare
scroll from the start (the bot's whole run with the boss is ≈ 225 s):

| Camera x | Time | Section |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 s | **The shallows** (checkpoint 0): bead popcorn streams, splitting `froth` bubbles, `brood-bubble`s with a `gill-dart` inside, `gill-dart` formations, carriers (`tender`, zone A's capsule carrier) |
| 1,800 – 3,800 | 40 – 88 s | **The reef tunnel** (checkpoint 2,000): a floor and a ceiling of reef; `urchin` turrets on both, more beads, fish and brood bubbles |
| 3,800 | 88 s | **SPUME HERALD** (SH-02) — a `boss` event, no WARNING: a captain fighting while the view scrolls on, for at most 30 s |
| 4,600 – 6,800 | 110 – 156 s | **The deep current** (checkpoint 4,600): `reef-jelly` ring-firers, froth formations, urchins — and the **hidden bonus entrance** (armed at 5,220, [below](#the-hidden-bonus-stage-pearl-grotto)) |
| 6,800 – 8,600 | 156 – 177 s | **The riptide** (checkpoint 6,800): the scroll speeds up to 1.4 px/tick; fish formations, beads, jellies |
| 8,600 – 9,300 | 177 – 193 s | **The calm**: exactly two carriers (the capsules for the boss) |
| 9,300 | ≈ 3:13 | The WARNING, then **GALVANIC MAW** (GM-02) |

**Wavy water.** The parallax has the far nebula band `bg/brine-nebula` and the sea band
`bg/brine-sea`; the stage's `raster` list puts a `wave` on each (the sea: rows 152–200, amplitude
3, wavelength 18, period 100; the nebula: rows 24–88, amplitude 2) and its `cycles` list rolls the
sea's four colours. The sea is painted **only** in the four colours of `BRINE_RAMP` — the exact
colours the palette cycle names — so the cycle recolours all of it (M2-08's `bg/sea-swell`
recipe; `procedural-zones.test.ts` pins it).

### SPUME HERALD (SH-02)

The mid-boss the plan asks for in zone B (zone C has none — the plan names one only for B): a
captain (`role: 'captain'`, M2-09) built on the existing `captain.launcher` — no new behaviour.
Parts: the `shell` (core, 40 hp; `bosses/herald-shell`) and two launch tubes (guns, 10 hp and 500
points each, drawn with `bosses/maw-pod`). It tracks the player, fires aimed 3-ways every 85 ticks
at 1.2 and launches its `minion`, the `brood-bubble`, from both tubes every 120 ticks. Its
`timeLimit` is 1,800 ticks: after 30 s of fighting it escapes to the right (a captain's escape sets
no ending flag). 4,000 points.

| 3 | — | track 0.55, shut 100 / open 110, 5-way cutters (spread 40) every 30 at 1.5, rings of 10 at 1.1, rockets every 110, `gape` 9 |

The gapes were 4 / 4 / 5 until M2-18: opened 4 px, the jaws' hurtboxes left an 18-px gap — exactly
the height of a fully powered MANTA's HUGE DISC (half height 9), which always touched a jaw first,
clinked on the armour and died, so the release route runs could not beat the boss. At 8 / 8 / 9 the
gap between the open jaws is taller than the biggest Direct-mode disc in every phase
(`test/integration/release-content-edge.test.ts` holds that relation;
[release-hardening.md](release-hardening.md#what-the-checks-found-and-the-fixes)).

**Homing rockets are minions.** `BossScriptApi` has no homing-bullet call; the rocket is the
boss's `minion` (`maw-rocket`, `rocket.homing`), launched from a pod with `api.launch(part)` like a
captain's minion. So the rockets are shootable (1 hp, 100 points), count as enemies (the kill
rate, the bot's scan), and the route needed no engine change. Their speed (1.25 px/tick), turn cap
and time limit keep them dodgeable with four directions; the content test holds them to the
2 px/tick limit like aimed bullets.

### The hidden bonus stage (PEARL GROTTO)

One `gap` entrance (M2-10's framework): the `bonus` event at x 5,220 arms a region at the very top
of the view — world x 5,616–5,680, y 0–24 — into `brine-grotto`. Two 16×24 reef **blocks**
(`block` events at 5,200, `screenX` 400 and 480) mark it: they stand at the top on either side of
the gap, so a ship must fly under the left block and then up into the gap while it passes. The
content test checks that the gap is marked by its blocks and leads into a bonus stage with bonus
capsules and a 1UP.

`brine-grotto.stage.json` — **PEARL GROTTO**, `type: 'bonus'`, 1,800 px at 1 px/tick (≈ 30 s): a
reef floor and ceiling over the brine sea, the bonus vault's carriers (`vault-carrier` formations

`PrepareStage`, so it plays through the zone's resident set (M2-10's gotcha) — its `music` names
the same `Stage` / `Boss` cues. Everything else — the warp after 40 ticks, the lock-out after a
death, the clear counting as the zone's clear (GALVANIC MAW skipped, run flag `bonus`) — is
M2-10's framework, unchanged ([campaign-and-bonus-stages.md](campaign-and-bonus-stages.md#hidden-bonus-stages)).

## The new behaviours (`core/behaviors`)

All four follow the coroutine rules of D29 (sleep until the next thing to do, whole-tick timers,
no allocation per wake). Defaults in brackets; the zone content overrides many of them.

### `rocket.homing`

A homing rocket. It launches diagonally away from the playfield's middle row and to the left —
up-left above the middle, down-left below it (the middle row itself: down-left) — at
[`launchSpeed` 1] px/tick for [`launchTicks` 24] ticks (`Straight` mover), then homes on the
nearest living player at [`speed` 1.25] turning at most [`turnRate` 5] binary units a tick for
[`homeTicks` 60] ticks (`Homing` mover), then flies straight on until it leaves the view. The
straight run is a `Homing` mover with turn rate 0: the mover derives its heading from the
velocity it had, so the course never jumps. It never fires. Times below 1 count as 1 tick,
fractions are floored, a negative turn rate is 0.

### `worm.burst`

A segment of a sand worm (`shmup_feat.md` §11 "segmented worms"). A `formation` of floor enemies is
one worm:

- the **leader** (member 0, or a lone spawn) gets a `Ballistic` mover with a proximity trigger
  [`trigger` 128] px (0 = at once) — it lies still in the sand until the nearest living player is
  that close horizontally, then flies from (`vx` −0.8, −`up` 3.4) px/tick with [`gravity` 0.075]
  and at most [`maxFall` 4], landing `BallisticLand.Pass` — it passes through the terrain, dives
  back into the ground and leaves the view;
- every **other member** gets `Follow`: it replays the leader's recorded track, so the body rises
  out of the same hole segment by segment.

Ground bodies move in the world, so the arc is fixed to its dune while the stage scrolls. A killed
or departed leader keeps recording as a ghost (`core/enemies`), so the rest of the worm still
follows the arc. It never fires. The shipped worms use `trigger` 190 — see the
[bot gotcha](#gotchas).

### `boss.maw`

GALVANIC MAW's script. Tunables: [`trackSpeed` 0.4, `margin` 44] (follows the nearest player's
height, `margin` px inside the playfield), [`closedTicks` 140, `openTicks` 90] (the `whenOpen`
parts — the mouth — shut and open in turn, `setOpenAll`), [`fireTicks` 36, `ways` 3, `spread` 48,
`bulletSpeed` 1.4] (while open, each standing core fires an aimed spread of purple needles — the
cutters; the first volley 12 ticks after the mouth opens, none while shut), [`ring` 0 = none,
`ringSpeed` 1] (a ring of round red bullets from each core as the mouth opens, each ring turned
half a gap from the last), [`launchTicks` 150, `count` 1] (up to `count` standing guns in turn
launch the `minion`; 0 = none) and [`gape` 0 = still].

One script per phase that sleeps until the soonest of three timers (the mouth, the cutters, the
rockets); a timer that never runs is `NEVER_TICKS`, the fire timers go through `fireWait`
(rank-scaled). With `gape` > 0 every part attached to a core (its `parent` is a core) is placed
`gape` px farther from the core's row than its **rest offset** while the mouth is open — a part
above the core up, one below it down; a part on the core's row (rest 0) never moves — and back at
rest when it shuts. Only the y is placed; the jaws keep their x.

### `boss.widow`

SANDGRAVE WIDOW's script. Tunables: [`stepTicks` 100] with the box [`minX` 250, `maxX` 320, `minY`
56, `maxY` 144] (every step it `moveTo`s a random whole-pixel point of the box — the gameplay RNG —
in 60 % of the step; swapped bounds are put in order), [`fireTicks` 80, `ways` 3, `spread` 40,
`bulletSpeed` 1.3] (each standing core spits an aimed spread of red ovals — also while the core is
still armoured), [`launchTicks` 160, `count` 1] (drones from the guns in turn), [`laserTicks` 0 =
never] with [`laserLength` 384, `laserWidth` 6, `telegraph` 50, `active` 40] (every `laserTicks`,
rank-scaled, the next standing gun spins a silk line — but never sooner than a whole lane after the
last one: `⌈telegraph⌉ + LASER_GROW_TICKS + ⌈active⌉ + LASER_FADE_TICKS`, a negative telegraph or
active counting as 0, M2-18). It starts with `api.hold()` and sleeps until the soonest of its four
timers.

### Shared helpers

Module-private helpers in `behaviors/index.ts` any future boss script can reuse:

| Helper | What it does |
|---|---|
| `fireCores(api, ways, spread, speed, kind)` | An aimed `nWay` from every standing core part → bullets fired |
| `ringCores(api, count, speed, kind, offset)` | A ring from every standing core part → bullets fired |
| `launchFromGuns(api, next, count)` | Launches the boss's `minion` from up to `count` standing guns, in turn from part `next` → the next call's `next` |
| `setJaws(api, apart)` | Places every part attached to a core `apart` px beyond its rest `restY` (0 = at rest) |

### `BossPart.restX` / `restY` (the jaw fix)

`BossPart` has two fields since M2-11: `restX` / `restY`, the part's offsets from its parent **as
in the boss data**, set with `localX` / `localY` when the boss is activated (and reset for the next
boss in the slot). `setPartOffset` still moves the part wherever a behaviour says; a behaviour that
moves a part and wants it back measures from the rest offsets, never from the current `localX` /
`localY`.

Why: the first version moved the jaws *relative* to where they stood and undid "the new phase's
gape" at a phase start — but the jaws had been moved by the *previous* phase's gape. GALVANIC MAW's
gapes were then 4, 4, 5 (8, 8, 9 since M2-18), so in phase 3 the jaws sat at ±8 shut and ±13 open instead of ±9 and ±14, and
their hurtboxes moved with them (review round 1). Now every phase starts with `setJaws(api, 0)` —
shut and at rest — and every move is absolute, so no phase can inherit another's drift.
`behaviors-zones.test.ts` pins the shut jaws at the data's −9 / 9 after both phase changes and the
open jaws exactly one gape beyond in each phase.

- **`dune.mjs`** (`DUNE_SPRITES`): `bg/dune-suns` (`SUNS_TILE_W` 72 × `SUNS_TILE_H` 40, placed
  with a wide `spacing`), `bg/dune-ridge` (`RIDGE_TILE_W` 128 × `RIDGE_TILE_H` 40, ridge profiles
  summed from triangle waves whose periods divide the tile width — seamless), the enemies
  `dune-worm`, `husk-crawler`, `sand-skimmer`, `dust-devil`, `sand-geyser`, `sand-clod`,
  `widow-drone` and the boss parts `widow-body`, `widow-head`, `widow-fang`, `widow-leg-top` /
  `-bottom` (mirrors), `widow-spinneret`.
- **`common.mjs`** gained two shape helpers: `fillEllipse(image, cx, cy, rx, ry, paint)` (asks
  `paint(u, v, e, x, y)` for the colour of every pixel whose centre is inside the ellipse — `u` /
  `v` in radii, `e` the normalised distance; `null` leaves the pixel) and `drawLine(image, x0, y0,
  x1, y1, rgba, width = 1)` (a line of round dots — legs, spikes, tentacles).
- **`terrain.mjs`** draws three tilesets from `TERRAIN_PALETTES` (`tiles/terrain-a` unchanged,
  `tiles/terrain-reef` — pale coral over deep blue-grey stone —, `tiles/terrain-dune` — sand):
  the same tiles, frames and shapes, only the rock colours differ (the texture hash is seeded per
  set); bricks, cubes and tissue look the same in every set. The tileset files are copies of
  `terrain-a.tileset.json` naming the other sprite.

Every sprite that can be hit has its `@flash` sibling; geometry uses only `+ − × ÷` and
`Math.sqrt`, randomness the seeded asset RNG and `hash2`, so the pixels are identical on every
engine. Both generators are registered in `procedural/index.mjs`. Colours stay low in saturation
next to the bullets and capsules (`shmup_feat.md` §12, §18).

## Building the next zone (M2-12 … M2-14)

The recipe these two zones followed:

1. **Stage** `content/stages/zone-<x>.stage.json` over the stub: 3–6 minutes (2.5–4.5 minutes of
   scroll to the WARNING), ≥ 4 checkpoints, a high-speed key, a calm with two carriers, its own
   `tilemap.tileset`, `parallax` bands, optional `raster` / `cycles`, a `directItems` plan. A
   `ground` bonus entrance needs every ground enemy of an earlier event off screen when its window
   arms (M2-13 — [zones-f-and-g.md](zones-f-and-g.md#the-prism-gallery-and-glimmer-cache); the
   content test checks it).
2. **Roster** `content/enemies/zone-<x>.enemies.json` (sorts after zone A's — spec indices of
   earlier files never move): 4–6 new types by sprite, existing behaviours where they fit, bosses
   with three phases; every aimed speed ≤ 2 px/tick. Paths and DSL patterns in their folders.
3. **Behaviours** only for what the existing ones cannot do; a boss that moves parts measures from
   `restX` / `restY`; a homing threat is a minion, not a bullet.
4. **Art** in a new `scripts/assets/procedural/<zone>.mjs` registered in `procedural/index.mjs`
   (hit flashes on everything hittable), a `TERRAIN_PALETTES` entry plus a tileset file.
5. **Songs** `content/audio/music/zone-<x>` / `boss-<x>` with `"stages": ["zone-<x>"]`.
6. **Checks**: extend the `zones` table of the content test — since M2-12 with the zones a run can
   have flown before (`earlier`) and the boss's lane limit (`lanes`) — (or a new test for new archetypes),
   add `test/playtest/zone-<x>.test.ts` and the zone's checkpoints to a recovery test, golden
   replays `zone-<x>-god` (+ a no-god run), a browser spec for the backdrop and the boss.
7. **Re-bless** the goldens whose hashes move (new sprites / scripts shift ids) — with the reason
   in the commit message — and watch the bundle budget.

## Gotchas

| Symptom | Cause / fix |
|---|---|
| The 4-way bot dies to a rising worm | The bot does not predict the vertical motion of ground enemies. The shipped worms trigger at 190 px so the arc comes down in front of the ship's column; a shorter trigger lets the head rise into the bot. Deaths without god mode are reported, not asserted |
| A boss part drifts a pixel after a phase change | It was moved relative to its current `localX` / `localY`. Measure from `restX` / `restY` and start every phase from rest (`setJaws(api, 0)`) |
| The playtest times the captain instead of the boss | Read the main encounter (a slot with `role === BossRole.Boss`), not `bosses.boss` — a captain can take slot 0 |
| "4–6 new enemy types" fails after adding a ceiling variant | Types are counted by sprite: a floor and a ceiling variant with the same sprite are one type |
| Every golden fails after adding a sprite or a behaviour | The sorted sprite and script ids moved; re-bless (`pnpm golden:update`) and check that inputs, ticks and outcomes are unchanged |
| The brine sea does not cycle its colours | A pixel outside `BRINE_RAMP` (or a ramp that no longer matches the stage's `cycles`) — the palette cycle only recolours exact matches |
| The bonus stage plays AZURE VERGE's theme | Expected when it is played alone (`?stage=brine-grotto`): no track is scoped to its id, so the defaults play. Inside a run it plays zone B's resident set (a bonus warp prepares nothing) |
| The gap entrance never opens | The ship must be `alive` inside world x 5,616–5,680, y 0–24 while the camera is between the event's `x` and `until` — fly under the left block, then up |
| The widow's head takes no damage | By design: `afterParts` of both fangs. The fangs stand in its lane |
| Shots clink on GALVANIC MAW | The mouth is shut (`whenOpen`), or they hit the hull or the jaws (armour) |
| The Tizen bundle check fails on size | 307.5 of 350 KB gzip after M2-11; big content (songs, stages) is inlined — trim or wait for M2-17's budget work |
