# Boss Deaths — MEGA CRASH Shockwave

The screen-wide white expanding ring that ends a captain for good — the punctuation mark
after the chain, after the gold. Two scales of the same event: clean arcade ring, and the
dirty version that shoves the whole field.

**Model:** ltx-video
**Mode:** t2v
**Variation:** 1/2
