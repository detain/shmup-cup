GL 1 vs 2 calls a scenario’s difference only beyond the **p90** spread *and* with the two versions’ run ranges apart; the overall shift (the median of the per-scenario differences) only beyond the run-to-run **drift**. Two-tick share is the stable metric; synced p95 is the noisy one.
